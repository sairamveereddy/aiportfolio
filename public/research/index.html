The Invisible Singularity: Artificial Intelligence as Convergent Infrastructure and the Reorganization of Global Work, 2027–2035

Author: Sairam Kumar Reddy Veereddy
Published: July 14, 2026 · Zenodo (CERN) · Open Access (CC BY 4.0)

Argues that rapid AI model proliferation is a transitional phase preceding structural convergence into unremarkable economic infrastructure. Develops the Acceleration and Reinstatement Hypotheses, projecting that task-creation dynamics will drive net employment growth in newly instantiated occupational categories.

Preprint / Working Paper DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21367111

From Detection to Proficiency: The Coming Inversion of Academic AI Policy and a Framework for Assessing Student AI Utilization in Higher Education (2026–2035)

Author: Sairam Kumar Reddy Veereddy
Published: July 15, 2026 · Zenodo (CERN) · Open Access (CC BY 4.0)

Demonstrates why detection-based institutional AI policies in academia are structurally unsustainable. Introduces AI Utilization Proficiency (AUP) across six assessable dimensions and proposes Baseline Differential Analysis (BDA) to infer usage quality from student-submitted artifacts.

Open Scientific Archiving and Citation Standards

The publications listed above represent independent research preprints permanently archived on Zenodo, an open-science platform operated by CERN and supported by the European Commission. Each record is assigned a persistent Digital Object Identifier (DOI), cataloged in DataCite, and distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licenses.

All papers provide machine-readable metadata formatted for Google Scholar, standard BibTeX, APA, and IEEE citation engines, and are accompanied by direct PDF files and open GitHub code/data repositories.
